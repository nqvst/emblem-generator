FAQ

Is the Halo 3 Emblem Generator free?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser, there is no account, and downloads are free.

Can I use my emblem as a profile picture?

Yes. The 512×512 PNG works well as a Discord, Steam, Xbox or forum avatar, or as a clan logo.

Can I hide the secondary colour, like in Halo 3?

Yes. Untick "Show secondary layer" to get the same result as the in-game toggle.

Are these the official emblems?

The artwork is a high-resolution fan recreation of the Halo 3 emblems, so it stays sharp at large sizes.
